Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about pricing, memberships, and what to expect on cleaning day.

How much does window cleaning cost?

Every home is different — your price depends on the size of your home, how many windows and French panes you have, and when they were last cleaned. Get a free quote in about 30 seconds and we’ll get back to you with your exact price.

Which cleaning do I need?

Exterior Only cleans every outside window. Inside & Out adds the interior glass and screens. Full Detail also includes track detailing and hard water spot removal — great for move-ins, move-outs, and windows that haven’t been cleaned in a while. Not sure? We’ll recommend one with your quote.

What are French panes?

French pane windows are split into many small panes by a grid. Each little pane is cleaned on its own, so they take extra time — that’s why we ask about them when you request a quote.

How do memberships work?

Pick Monthly, Quarterly, or Biannual and save $150, $100, or $50 on every cleaning. Monthly and Quarterly also come with three free bonuses: Shine Guard Pro Tech, our 7-Day Rain Guarantee, and hard water removal. We schedule every visit for you, and one-time cleanings are always available too.

What if it rains right after my cleaning?

Monthly and Quarterly members are covered by our 7-Day Rain Guarantee: if it rains within 7 days of your cleaning, we come back and re-clean for free.

Do I need to be home?

For exterior cleanings, no — as long as we can safely reach your windows (gates unlocked and pets inside). For interior cleanings, someone will need to be home to let us in.

Is it safe for my family and pets?

Yes. We use pet-friendly, family-safe cleaning solutions, and we always clean up after ourselves.

What areas do you serve?

We’re based in North Phoenix and serve the whole metro area, including Phoenix, Scottsdale, Arcadia, Cave Creek, Fountain Hills, Tempe, Mesa, Chandler, Gilbert, and Ahwatukee. Not sure if we cover you? Give us a call.
